# Heinlein's Rules for Writers

**Presentation handout draft · A Publishing Company of One**

Robert A. Heinlein's five often-cited rules, paraphrased for this handout:

1. Write.
2. Finish what you start.
3. Don't endlessly rewrite, except when working from specific editorial direction.
4. Submit your writing to a market.
5. Keep submitting it until it sells.

These are Heinlein's professional writing rules, **not** the Heinlein software's automated editorial checks. In a modern independent-publishing practice, rule four might mean releasing work through your own imprint, while rule five is about persistence and finding readers.

**The talk's point:** The publishing pipeline can accelerate editing, design, packaging, verification, and distribution. It doesn't substitute for the time spent actually writing the story.

For the presentation, print this as a separate one-page handout or book insert. Attribute the rules to Robert A. Heinlein and describe this wording as a paraphrase, not a verbatim quotation.
